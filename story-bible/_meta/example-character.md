## Overview

Protagonist. Wants vindication; needs to forgive herself. Drives the expedition plot from the
inciting incident onward.

## Traits

- Left-handed; always sketches with charcoal — a visible tell across scenes.
- Scar through the right eyebrow from the first expedition.
- Speaks in clipped, precise measurements when afraid.

## Relationships

- Estranged brother — hostile at first; thaws after the mountain pass.
- The expedition captain — an employer who does not know her history.

## Arc

Believes the lie that she is only her worst mistake; learns the truth that a map can be redrawn.

## Knowledge

- Opening → midpoint — does not know her brother survived the first expedition.
- After the pass — learns the sabotage was not entirely her fault.
